MAGNETICIAN

Magnets: the original wireless.

You are the magician of magnets. Place bar magnets, flip their poles, then release the steel orb and watch physics do the show. Land it in the cradle.

● Red pushes.  ● Blue pulls.

HOW TO PLAY

Tap a magnet in the tray to drop it on the board. Drag it to move it. Tap a placed magnet to flip its poles. Double-tap to send it back.

Press RELEASE to let the orb fly. RESET stops the run and keeps your layout. Match or beat par for a star.

Coils fire the orb. Gray blocks wake up near magnets and join in. Pulsing magnets keep their own rhythm. Blue shimmer slows everything down.
